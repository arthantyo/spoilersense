# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spoiler Senser handles information when the App is installed or used in a subreddit or other community.

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on your configuration, the App may process:

- Post and comment text
- Post and comment metadata
- Username and author information
- Subreddit or community information
- Moderation decisions and action results
- Configuration values needed to operate the App, such as API keys and webhook URLs

The App may also transmit selected content to third-party services you configure, including language model providers and notification services.

## 2. How We Use Information

The App uses the information above to:

- Detect likely spoilers
- Apply or suggest moderation actions
- Send moderator alerts
- Create moderation comments or notes when enabled by the App's logic
- Improve reliability and debugging of the moderation workflow

## 3. Sharing of Information

The App does not intentionally sell personal information.

Information may be shared with:

- Reddit services necessary to read content and perform moderation actions
- Language model providers when a configured moderation check requires them
- Alerting services such as Discord or Slack when you enable those integrations

Those third-party services may process the data according to their own policies.

## 4. Data Retention

The App only retains information for as long as necessary to perform moderation, logging, and operational purposes, or as required by the platform and hosting environment.

Any retention outside the App's direct control may be governed by Reddit, your hosting provider, or the third-party services you configure.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect the App and its configuration. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting any API keys, webhook URLs, or similar credentials you configure for the App.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Your Choices

Community moderators control whether the App is installed and how it is configured. If you want the App to stop processing community data, uninstall it or remove its access from the relevant subreddit settings.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will apply when it is posted in the repository or wherever the App is distributed.

## 9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the App maintainer or project owner through the repository or hosting channel where the App is distributed.
